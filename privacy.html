Privacy Policy

Aligner Tracker · last updated 9 September 2026
The short version: this app collects nothing. Everything you enter stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising.

What the app stores

Aligner Tracker keeps the information you enter — tray numbers and dates, how long the aligner is in or out of your mouth, appointments, notes, comfort scores, elastics details and any lost or broken trays you record — in your device's local storage.

Where that information goes

Nowhere. It is not transmitted to the developer or to anyone else. There is no server component, no user account and no login. Nobody but you can see it.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising or tracking identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device so it can tell you when to put an aligner back in or change trays. These are created and delivered entirely by your phone. No push service is used and nothing is sent over the internet.

Backups you make yourself

The app can export a backup file of your data. Once you have exported it, that file is yours to look after — wherever you choose to save or send it is outside the app's control.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Erase everything deletes all of it immediately. Deleting the app removes it too. Because nothing is held anywhere else, there is nothing for you to request from the developer and nothing for the developer to delete.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear at this address with a new date at the top.

Not medical advice

Aligner Tracker is a personal timer and record keeper. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical or dental advice. Follow the instructions your orthodontist gives you.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address listed on the app's App Store page.
